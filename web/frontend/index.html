Solar Boat UPRM Team

Who We Are

Engineering students.
Representing Puerto Rico.

We are a student team from the College of Engineering at the University of Puerto Rico, Mayagüez Campus (UPRM). We design, build, and race solar-powered boats.

We compete in international solar boat competitions, proudly representing Puerto Rico on the world stage through innovation and teamwork.
